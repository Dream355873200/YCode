# Soft 3D Render — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *soft. — Soft Landing* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `04-3d-render`

## Story & structure

Pastel infinite cyc, 24 fps with motion blur and shallow DOF (f/1.8 feel). A MoGraph cloner field of glossy rounded pills ripples with an effector wave → an inflated 3D wordmark drops in, jiggles on a damped spring, collides with and displaces the field → camera arcs as materials show off (SSS gummy, glossy plastic, one chrome accent) → long-tail ease into a clean hero composition held ~1 s.

## Technique notes

- Long-tail easing: ~80 % of each move in the first 20 % of its time.
- Damped-spring jiggle on impacts, ~8–10 decaying oscillations.
- Material trio: SSS candy (pink/peach), glossy plastic (cream/lilac), frosted glass or chrome used once as jewellery.
- ASMR sound design: soft thuds, squishes, bubbly pops, frame-synced; airy pad underneath.
- R3F note: approximate SSS with MeshPhysicalMaterial transmission/thickness + rim glow; real SSS is out of budget.

## Sound

ASMR soft thuds/squishes/pops over an airy cinematic pad.
