# Frame-by-Frame Cel Boil — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *HAND MADE* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `05-cel-boil`

## Story & structure

Warm paper, ink lines boiling on twos. A matchstick strikes (SMEAR frame on 1s) → a flame spirit jumps out with anticipation + squash → dances on 2s → bursts into cel FX: curling smoke, sparks, star bursts, speed lines → FX re-form into a hand-lettered title that keeps boiling on a 3s hold.

## Technique notes

- Boil: redraw line control points with slight variation every 2 frames (Turbulent-Displace logic: amount 2–5 %, size ~50) — **seed-stepped variants, never continuous noise**.
- Fills sit 1–2 px off-register, re-rolled on twos; paper grain multiply 10–15 %.
- Smear frames: fast moves elongate the subject 150–300 % for a single frame on 1s; holds go 3s.
- Cel FX accent layer: 3–4 frame hand-drawn cycles for smoke/sparks/speed lines, sharing the exact 3–4 colour palette (ink, tomato, yellow, cream).

## Sound

Playful jazzy pizzicato/xylophone; strike/fwoosh/pop/poof/sparkle cut on the same stepped grid.
