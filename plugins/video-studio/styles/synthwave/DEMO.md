# 80s Synthwave / VHS — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *NEON DRIVE — Midnight 1986* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `10-synthwave`

## Story & structure

Starry night, striped retro sun, wireframe mountains, palm silhouettes, neon grid rushing to the vanishing point. 0–1 s VHS tracking noise + `PLAY ▶` OSD → 1–4 s camera flies low over the grid as the sun rises → 4–6 s CHROME title slams in with a lens flare on a big chord → 6–7.5 s pink neon script writes on like a tube with flicker → 7.5–10 s sustained ride, VHS chroma bleed/scanlines, end on the hero frame.

## Technique notes

- Trinity: one-point perspective grid with looped vertical scroll; banded sun (horizontal cut-outs, magenta→orange→yellow); double glow (tight ~4 px core + wide ~30 px halo).
- Chrome type: 5–8 stop metallic gradient + bevel + one star-glint sweep.
- VHS chain: RGB split ±1–2 px → scanlines 2 px @10 % → 1–2 random glitch hitches per second → optional 4:3 rounded mask; stutter to 15 fps for 2 frames occasionally.
- Purple sky gradient; magenta/cyan neon; the warm sun band is what dates it to 1986.

## Sound

Gated-reverb snare, arpeggiated saw bass, lush pads, tape hiss; big chord + crash on the slam.
