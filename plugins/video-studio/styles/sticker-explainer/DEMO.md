# Sticker Explainer — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *How Many Elements Hide in a Phone?* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `19-paperclip`

## Story & structure

Calm blue-grey canvas `#E9EEF2` + slate, one accent `#FF5A36`. One continuous camera path: sticker phone photo (white 10 px outline, soft shadow) → exploded-parts stickers with leader-line labels → periodic-table grid lighting up element tiles → bar chart growing with a count-up "70+" → world map with supply routes drawing → pull out to a bold typographic title card (heavy sans, huge number, small caption). Every beat introduces a new element; charts exact.

## Technique notes

- Sticker pipeline: photo cutout + 8–12 px white outline + soft shadow, ±3° rotate — equalises any source photo.
- One huge canvas, camera translate/scale on long eases; stations entered and exited cleanly, never a hard cut.
- Chart moves 500–800 ms (bar growth, count-up, leader draws) landed before the sentence ends.
- 2–3 colours + accent; equal-weight sans; seriousness is the brand.

## Sound

Clean tech-explainer bed; UI ticks on sticker landings, ticking during count-ups, soft whoosh between stations.
