# Line Art — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *ATELIER LINEA — One Line* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `02-line-art`

## Story & structure

One stroke tells a brand story: seed → sprouts → branches become a building's structural lines → outline extends into a skyline → the horizon line lifts and loops into a monogram → **line-to-fill** (logo fills gold from its anchor) → thin letter-spaced serif wordmark fades up. Camera follows the pen tip, then dollies out: the whole film resolves into a poster.

## Technique notes

- Single champagne-gold line `#E9D7A5`, constant ~3 px, round caps, on `#0B1320`; glowing tip leads the draw.
- Draw speed eases into corners: `cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`; the tail of each figure is the start of the next (path pre-joined end-to-start).
- Older segments dim to ~35 % as the camera moves on, keeping the tip brightest.
- Line-to-fill once, at the climax: fill expands from the path anchor in ~12 f, one chime.

## Sound

Minimal felt piano + string swell; pen-on-paper whisper tracking tip speed; delicate chime on the fill.
