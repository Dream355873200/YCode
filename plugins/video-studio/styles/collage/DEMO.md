# Collage Cut-out — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *NOGGIN Quarterly / 脑洞大开* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `06-collage`

## Story & structure

Newsprint/kraft ground. A Victorian portrait cutout (white scissor edge + halftone) stands centre; the top of the head FLIPS OPEN like a lid (Gilliam homage) revealing deep space, from which flowers, birds, planets, pointing hands and a vintage rocket spill out. Jaw flap talks with the sound; hands slap elements onto the page; torn-paper strips; bold constructivist red/black diagonal type; torn-paper wipe to the end card.

## Technique notes

- Cutouts: 2–4 px white scissor edge + halftone filter on tones + tiny hard shadows; ±2 px hand-placement jitter each step.
- Motion on 12 fps (holds 6 fps) — stepped, never eased; jaw is a separate 2–3 frame flap swap.
- Layer order: paper grain → colour blocks → photo cutouts → scribbles/tape/stamps → grain overlay.
- Transitions: hand-slap an element on, or a torn strip wipes the page.

## Sound

Vinyl crackle + jazzy boom-bap; paper rustles, scissors snips, slaps, a comic pop on the lid.
