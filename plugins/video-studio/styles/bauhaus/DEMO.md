# Geometric Bauhaus — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *KONSTRUKTION · 20 Beats* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `09-bauhaus`

## Story & structure

Cream paper `#F1E9DA`; primaries red/yellow/blue + black. 120 BPM, 0.5 s per beat, 240 px module grid. 0–2 s grid lines draw, first red circle lands → 2–6 s the poster assembles one action per beat (quarter-circles rotate 90° on corner pivots, semicircles slide one module, triangles flip, squares split) → 6–8 s phase-shifted cascade wave across the grid on the drop → 8–10 s freeze into the poster: "BAUHAUS" vertical in heavy geometric sans + small caps "FORM · FARBE · FUNKTION".

## Technique notes

- Every displacement an integer number of modules; every landing on a grid line; easing `power2.inOut` or linear — **no overshoot ever**.
- Rotation pivots alternate: own centre / corner vertex / frame centre.
- Cascade: same action rippling with 1-beat offsets — the style's only fluency.
- Subtle paper tooth + ≤1 px print misregistration allowed.

## Sound

Minimal techno/click: pitched blip per action, kick on quarters, a drop under the cascade.
