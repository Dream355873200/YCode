# Flat Vector — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *popwise — One Dot* (10 s) · mg-styles-15 `01-flat-vector`

## Story & structure

One continuous shot, no cuts. A saturated circle drops into an empty bold field with full elastic craft (0–1.2 s hook) → becomes the sun; a flat city pops up building-by-building with 2–4 f stagger → camera pushes into one window: a geometric character at a desk reacts to a phone ping → the notification bubble expands into a full-frame shape wipe → icons burst in rhythm (bar grows, check draws, heart pops, coin flips) → everything collapses into a logo lockup + tagline, elastic settle, 1 s hold with micro-motion.

## Technique notes

- Three-phase entries everywhere: wind-up 2–3 f → overshoot 110–115 % → settle ~4 f; `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)`.
- Character rig: limb anchor rotations, head bob, blink cycle; squash & stretch ~10 %.
- The shape-wipe: the notification bubble scales until it covers frame, next chapter sits inside it.
- 5–6 flat colours, one hero per beat; end lockup holds with a blinking cursor.

## Sound

Upbeat future-bass ~120 BPM; pop/boing/whoosh frame-synced to every entry.
